Shiki 4.6.0: licences of the language grammars and colour themes AgentMux bundles

@shikijs/langs and @shikijs/themes 4.6.0 are generated from tm-grammars 1.32.22
and tm-themes 1.12.12, whose NOTICE files list each grammar's and theme's
upstream licence. This file holds the sections of those NOTICE files for the
grammars and themes in AgentMux's bundle, followed by sections for the bundled
grammars they don't cover. scripts/third-party-notices.mjs fails a release
build if a bundled grammar or theme has no section here, or if the installed
Shiki isn't the version on the first line.

To update after a Shiki upgrade, or when the editor bundles another grammar:
take the tm-grammars and tm-themes versions from that Shiki release's
pnpm-lock.yaml, `npm pack` both, and copy the sections the build names.
